## Step 4 + deep dive: broader silent-merge sweep

The brief flagged one known failure mode (a capitalized noun missing its article, with no inline plural marker, silently merging into the *previous* entry's examples — e.g. `Achtung` into `Absender`). I found that exact case immediately, and then discovered the same underlying parser weakness (the `isLowercaseStart` heuristic) actually has a **broader failure signature**: it also misfires whenever a headword line does *not* start with a plain uppercase letter — i.e. multi-word headwords (`was für ein`, `wie viel`, `weh tun`, `sich kümmern`, `an/auf/aus/weg/zu sein`, `der, die, das`), parenthetical-prefixed headwords (`(sich) anmelden`, `(sich) waschen`, `(Kredit)-Karte`), and hyphen-wrapped headwords spanning a line/column break (`Anrufbeantworter`, `Lebensmittel`). It also misfires the *other direction*: a mid-sentence line-wrap that happens to start with a lowercase word (`etwas`, `und`, `aus`, `sind.`, `bestellen`, `ein.`, `zweiten`) gets treated as a brand-new bogus entry instead of a continuation, truncating the real entry's example.

Because this signature is mechanically detectable, I ran full-file sweeps (not just 2–3 random sections) rather than purely random sampling, then visually verified a sample of the findings against PyMuPDF-rendered PDF pages (8, 10, 12, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 26 — covering sections A, B, D, E, K, L, M, N, P, R, S, T, V, W, Z). Sections **M/N (page 19)** and **Z (page 26)** were spot-checked and found clean (no silent-merge issues found there beyond the already-documented, accepted `wortart` guess weakness, e.g. `zufrieden`/`zusammen`/`zwischen` guessed as `"verb"` because they end in `-en` — expected/accepted, not corrected).

## Verification methodology
- `pdftoppm` (poppler) was not installed; installed `pymupdf` via pip (network access confirmed available) and rendered the specific PDF pages in question at 3x scale for visual, ground-truth confirmation of every non-trivial correction above — not just text-layer re-extraction, since I wanted to rule out any layout/rendering ambiguity.
- Also cross-checked via `pdftotext` (available at `/mingw64/bin/pdftotext`), both `-layout` and plain mode, against the Task-1-approved `wortliste_raw.txt`, confirming the raw text file itself is a faithful (if occasionally hyphen/parenthesis-fragmenting) transcription — none of the issues found here are Task 1 raw-text bugs; they are all parser-level (Task 2) misclassifications of already-correct raw text.
- Ran full-file automated sweeps against `wordlist.json` (not just the 2–3 sections nominally required) for: (a) first-`beispiele`-element starting with a lowercase letter, (b) literal `(sich)`/`(Ehe-)` substrings appearing outside a `word` field, (c) duplicate `word` values, (d) last-`beispiele`-element not ending in typical sentence punctuation, (e) suspiciously short `beispiele` elements. Each hit was individually checked against the raw text and/or a rendered PDF page before deciding fix vs. false-positive.
